Pineapple Mint
Green Cooler

Bright, cold, a little tart.

You need

  • 1 scoopAvoKind® Green Boost
  • ¾ cupcold water
  • ½ cupfrozen pineapple
  • 5–6mint leaves
  • ½lime, juiced
  • 1 handfulice

Make it

  1. 01

    Add everything to a blender.

  2. 02

    Blend until smooth and frosty.

  3. 03

    Taste. Add a little more lime if you want it brighter.

Green Yogurt
Crunch Bowl

Creamy, crunchy, and very hard to mess up.

You need

  • 1 scoopAvoKind® Green Boost
  • ¾ cupplain Greek or plant yogurt
  • 1–2 tbspmilk or water
  • ½ cupberries or sliced fruit
  • 2 tbspgranola
  • 1 tspchia seeds

Make it

  1. 01

    Stir Green Boost into the yogurt.

  2. 02

    Add just enough liquid to make it creamy.

  3. 03

    Top with fruit, granola, and chia.

04Breakfast5 min + overnight

Apple Ginger
Overnight Oats

Mix tonight. Grab tomorrow.

You need

  • 1 scoopAvoKind® Green Boost
  • ½ cuprolled oats
  • ¾ cupmilk or plant milk
  • ½green apple, grated
  • 1 tspchia seeds
  • ¼ tspcinnamon

Make it

  1. 01

    Whisk Green Boost into the milk.

  2. 02

    Stir in oats, apple, chia, and cinnamon.

  3. 03

    Cover overnight. Stir before eating.
