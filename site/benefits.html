Why avocado

It is part of the formulation logic, not a mascot.

Avocado contributes whole-food fat and texture. Dietary fat also participates in the absorption of fat-soluble compounds. Human meal studies have found higher absorption of several carotenoids when avocado or avocado oil was eaten with vegetables.

Important boundary

Those studies support the general food-science rationale. Green Boost itself has not been clinically tested for a measured increase in nutrient absorption.

Why these twelve

Each ingredient has a formula role. Research is kept in context.

The ingredient list stays readable. Where research exists, we distinguish studies of foods or concentrated extracts from evidence about Green Boost itself.

Pineapple

Formula role: bright fruit flavor.

Bromelain research usually uses concentrated preparations, not ordinary food amounts.

Nopal

Formula role: whole-food fiber and body.

Human studies often use much larger servings than a multi-ingredient powder.

Green apple

Formula role: tartness and fruit balance.

Whole apples provide fiber and polyphenols; food research is not finished-product proof.

Cucumber

Formula role: cool, fresh green note.

Its clearest role here is sensory, not a therapeutic claim.

Avocado

Formula role: texture and whole-food fat.

Human meal studies support the broader fat-and-carotenoid absorption mechanism.

Spinach

Formula role: leafy-green base.

Spinach contains carotenoids and dietary nitrate; intervention studies use specific doses.

Celery

Formula role: crisp green depth.

Plant-compound research does not establish a Green Boost health outcome.

Cilantro

Formula role: fresh herbal lift.

Used here primarily for whole-food flavor and formula complexity.

Ginger

Formula role: warm finish.

Digestive research generally studies dedicated ginger doses, not this formula.

Turmeric

Formula role: earthy spice.

Much turmeric research uses concentrated curcumin supplements; Green Boost uses whole turmeric.

Mint

Formula role: cool finish.

Clinical digestive studies often use concentrated peppermint oil, not whole mint in food.

Basil

Formula role: ties fruit and herbs together.

Here the direct value is flavor; preclinical ingredient research is not a product claim.

What it does

Freeze-drying removes most water under reduced pressure and relatively low product temperatures.

What it does not mean

It does not guarantee that every nutrient is unchanged. Retention varies by food, process, packaging, and storage.

Why AvoKind uses it

It turns perishable ingredients into a format that can live in a pouch and be mixed quickly.
