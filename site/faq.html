What is AvoKind Green Boost?

Green Boost is a freeze-dried whole-food smoothie powder made from 12 fruits, vegetables, herbs and roots. It is designed to mix with water, milk, or a plant-based drink without a blender.

What ingredients are in Green Boost?

Pineapple, nopal, green apple, cucumber, spinach, celery, avocado, cilantro, ginger, turmeric, mint, and basil.

Does Green Boost contain caffeine?

No. Green Boost is made from the listed whole-food ingredients and does not include coffee, tea, guarana, or added caffeine.

How do I prepare it?

Add one serving to cold water or your preferred milk, then stir or shake until mixed. You can also blend it into smoothies or use it in yogurt, oats, and recipes.

Why is avocado in the formula?

Avocado contributes a smooth mouthfeel and naturally occurring unsaturated fat. Dietary fat also plays a role in the absorption of fat-soluble compounds from foods, which is one reason avocado is an interesting partner for vegetables.

What does freeze-dried mean?

Freeze-drying removes water at low temperature and low pressure. It creates a shelf-stable food format that can be rehydrated quickly.

Is there added sugar?

No added sugar is used in Green Boost. Any sugars come from the whole-food ingredients themselves.
